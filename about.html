My Story

Travel Should Feel Like An Adventure — Not A Chore

Hi, I'm Shana Star Nunez, a dedicated travel advisor committed to creating exceptional travel experiences. As an independent, licensed travel agent based in Florida, I specialize in cruises, luxury vacations, all-inclusive resorts, and customized itineraries designed around your unique travel goals.

I started Stars Pack N Go Destinations because I believe everyone deserves a travel expert in their corner. From your first consultation until you return home, I provide personalized service and attention to every detail — hunting down the best value, handling the confusing fine print, and being a real person you can call or text before, during, and after your trip.

Travel is more than just taking a trip — it's about creating memories that last a lifetime. When you work with me, you're not just booking a vacation, you're getting a planning partner who genuinely wants every moment to be unforgettable, stress-free, and worth every penny.
